Fortuna Bay 3D — a sun-soaked 3D open-world beach city game on Isla Fortuna. Drive a living city at golden hour: 7 named districts, traffic and pedestrians, a 5-star FBPD wanted system with roadblocks and the FALCON-1 helicopter, 10 story missions plus taxi, courier and street-race side jobs, a 3-person crew (mechanic, hacker, decoy), a safehouse garage with respray and engine upgrades, hidden packages, a respect rank ladder, 3 synth radio stations, and passing rain showers that make the roads slick. Everything runs offline in the browser — Three.js, procedural textures, synthesized audio, no network requests.

## Features

- **Open world:** 14×14 block city on an island — 7 named districts, a marina, a port with cranes, a boardwalk, a lighthouse, 489 buildings with lit windows, and a 120 m Fortuna Tower.
- **Living traffic:** 26 AI cars that follow the grid, brake, and pull over for sirens; 42 pedestrians who walk, flee gunfire, and call the police when they witness a crime.
- **Wanted system:** 0–5 stars, FBPD pursuit with roadblocks and spike strips, the FALCON-1 helicopter with a searchlight, line-of-sight evasion, busted/respawn at FBPD HQ and the hospital.
- **Missions:** 10 story missions (steal, tail, chase, checkpoint race, rampage, survival, heist) plus taxi, courier, and street-race side jobs, with a beacon, objective banner, and timer.
- **Combat:** 5 weapons (fists, pistol, SMG, shotgun, rifle) with aim, hitscan, muzzle flash, and enemies that only shoot with line of sight.
- **Crew & garage:** Rico repairs your car, Vex scrubs a star, Lola sends a decoy; the safehouse heals you, stores cars, resprays, and upgrades engines.
- **Weather:** passing rain showers cut grip, pull the fog in, dim the sky, and play rain noise.
- **Save:** progress, cash, garage, and collected packages persist in localStorage.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — no build step, no install.

## Controls

WASD drive / walk · E enter car · M mission · 1–5 / Q weapons · Click shoot · Right-click aim · Space handbrake · Tab map · R radio · Z / X / C crew · G garage · Esc pause
